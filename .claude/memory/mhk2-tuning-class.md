**Why much downforce blew the physics up** (found on 2026-11-19 with `tune_races.py`, `runaway_look.py`)
- The game's downforce is `0.5 * (velocity · forward axis)²` along the kart's own down axis, in the air as well (code in [[mhk2-kart-parameters]]). For a stock kart that is 4.6 times gravity at 61 units/s.
- Stage 1, on the ground: with Anpressdruck / Gewicht * v² above about 45000 (for example 800 % / 50 % at 55 units/s, or 800 % / 100 % at 75) the suspension cannot hold the force within one 1/60 s step. The vertical speed starts to flip sign every frame, the amplitude grows, the collision code sets the kart back to its last position every other frame, and the kart leaves the ground.
- Stage 2, in the air: a tumbling kart is accelerated along its own down axis by a force that grows with the square of its speed. The speed doubled every frame; after a second it was 10^6 and more. The game keeps running, the kart is gone.
- Stage 2 also exists without stage 1: with ten times the thrust a kart stands up on its tail at the start, and then "down" points along the road. And a kart on its back is pushed upwards.
- Single values at their limits never did this (Anpressdruck 800 % alone tops out at 31 units/s); it took speed from Motorkraft and Übersetzung as well. Gewicht 25 % with Anpressdruck 800 % did it on a straight within a second.

**The three downforce rules of the class** (`downforce_cap`, hooked at 0x43FCE0 in place of `fld dword ptr [edi+264h]`; st0 = forward speed, only its square is used afterwards; constants `DOWNFORCE_SPEED`, `DOWNFORCE_SPEED_AIR`, `DOWNFORCE_UPRIGHT`)
1. The speed that goes into the downforce is limited to 120 * sqrt(Gewicht / Anpressdruck). So the downforce per kilogram never exceeds what a stock kart has at 120 units/s: 3.8 times the value of a 250 cc kart at its top speed, a third of where stage 1 began.
2. While none of the four wheels reported ground contact in this step (vehicle+0x4C, +0xD0, +0x154, +0x1D8) the limit is 75 * sqrt(...). The gearing of a 250 cc kart ends at 65 units/s, with the power can at 74.
3. While the y part of the kart's up axis is below 0.5 (tilted by more than 60 degrees; the up axis is the caller's [esp+3Ch], its y at [esp+4Ch] inside the routine) there is no downforce.
With the default values rules 1 and 2 never come into play, so the default tuning kart is the 250 cc kart except when it lies on its side or back. Other classes leave the routine at its first compare.
- Rule 1 alone was tried first: the speed no longer exploded, but karts that stood on their tail or flipped at the start were still pushed away at a constant 300 units/s² and left the track. Rule 3 as "on its back only" (sign of the up axis' y) left the tail-standing kart, which was shoved forward to over 200 units/s, past what its gearing allows (167).
